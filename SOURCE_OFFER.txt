ScorePlayer v0.8 source offer

This portable build is intended to be distributed together with the corresponding
ScorePlayer source archive and the exact HOMR source/version used by the build.

When publishing a release:
1. Attach ScorePlayer-Desktop-v0.8-source.zip.
2. Record the installed HOMR version (`python -m pip show homr`).
3. Provide the corresponding HOMR source as required by AGPL-3.0.
4. Preserve third-party license notices.
